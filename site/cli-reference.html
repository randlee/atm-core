atm peer trust migrate

Migrate or retire legacy literal-IP trusted-peer entries. Without `--yes`, prints the migration plan (one line per legacy literal-IP row: enabled/disabled, fingerprint, port, and the action) and makes no changes. With `--yes`, applies the plan: rows named by `--map IP=HOSTNAME` convert to the durable hostname, preserving their fingerprint and port; every other legacy literal-IP row is revoked.
