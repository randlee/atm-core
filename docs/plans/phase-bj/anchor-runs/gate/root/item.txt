# phase-bj: mechanical close flow: close hook, stack bead, sanity-stack, runs tabs

## Description

Repository: randlee/atm-bd-orchestration. Code, worktrees, sprint branches and PRs live there, on its `integrate/phase-bj`; atm-core's `integrate/phase-bj` holds only this plan. The rulings are Rand's of 2026-10-18, anchored as ANC-051..089 in `docs/plans/phase-bj/anchors.md`; the requirement docs are `docs/requirements/task-close-hook.md` and `docs/requirements/sanity-stack.md` of atm-bd-orchestration PR #342 with the dev, dev-sanity, team-lead and quality-mgr requirement items they amend.

### Goal
Dev work is not complete until sanity verifies it, and the lead does not need to intervene between a dev push and the QA assignment:
- one script, `atm-task-close.py --role <dev|fix|sanity|qa> --vars <file>`, is the only way a dev, fix, sanity or qa task completes; it checks the minimum, closes the bead, reports to the lead and assigns the next bead as the phase lead;
- the dev's ATM task stays open until the sanity close hook closes it on PASS; a sanity FAIL is one `sanity-fail.xml.j2` message to the dev on that open task, no finding children, counted in `metadata.sanity_round`, escalated to team-lead once the count exceeds `max_sanity_rounds` (default 5);
- the stack bead `<prefix>-phase-<x>.stack` is the ledger and the lock of the phase stack; the sanity side links the layer on PASS under its claim, first PASS wins the top and the other dev gets `rebase-required` without a round counted; a sanity-stack subagent handles one next-top layer per spawn, sequentially, with a lean mode and a lock timeout that never blocks the team and never escalates to a person;
- the pour writes branch, worktree, pr_target and difficulty onto every generated dev, fix, sanity and QA bead and validates the projected phase graph before writes;
- `atm-bd console` shows a sanity runs tab and a quality runs tab, newest first, QA run-level verdict and elapsed, the dev's name and icon, and an API_FAILURE kind distinct from a FAIL verdict.

Dev and fix assignment stay the lead's (ANC-060); atm-bd assigns only a sanity or qa bead that `bd ready` lists with no ATM task (ANC-061).

## Acceptance criteria

### Success Criteria
- A sanity PASS through the hook closes the dev's task as the lead and assigns QA as the lead; a sanity FAIL sends one `sanity-fail` message on the dev's open task and creates no bead (ANC-053, ANC-056, ANC-072).
- Two branches cut from the same top that both pass sanity: the first advances the stack bead from empty to singleton against trunk, the second is linked as the hosted top in `layers`; a conflicting second branch gets `rebase-required` and `sanity_round` does not move (ANC-065, ANC-067, ANC-071).
- Every sprint's QA PASS at its head; the stack lands on `integrate/phase-bj` of atm-bd-orchestration; `just lint` and `just test` green there.
- `atm-bd skill` verifies every installed file against the selected release manifest and refuses mismatches with the path list; `atm-hook-doctor.py` reports per-file integrity and aggregate PASS/FAIL from the install-recorded manifest.
